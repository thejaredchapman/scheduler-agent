The Front Desk

Ask for a meeting in plain words. Claude reads the calendars and pencils in a time and a room. Nothing is sent until you click Approve, and then plain code sends the invites, not the AI.

New request

Up to 500 characters. Name the people, how long, and any wishes, such as “Priya, Oliver and Hana, 30 minutes, any morning.”
